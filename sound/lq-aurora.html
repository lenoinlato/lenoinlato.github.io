Aurora

Líquen

March 2026

Recorded at Sá da Bandeira Studios & amparo99

Produced by Líquen

Mixed by Buga Lopes

Mastered by Miguel Pinheiro Marques

Liquen's latest single, "Aurora", samples a classic recording from Brigada Victor Jara not as nostalgia but as a living legacy and starting point, evoking collective memory and the power of popular song as an instrument of identity and resistance. Building upon this heritage, it constructs an immersive electronic landscape where the rhythmic pulse dialogues with verses that traverse time, from popular lyricism to direct social criticism. The song, conscious of the past and restless in the face of the present, portrays themes such as love, work, the weight of social expectations, and the domestication of desires. The result is a piece that respects its roots but rejects stagnation.
